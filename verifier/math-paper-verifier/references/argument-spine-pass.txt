ENSEMBLE_PAPER_AUDIT_PROTOCOL 2026-08-21.1 PASS argument-spine

Audit whether the paper's essential argument establishes every headline theorem.
This is a proof-architecture pass, not a counterexample search or an exhaustive
audit of remarks and side results.

For each formal headline theorem, record its exact quantified statement, ranges,
and location. Starting from its final deduction, trace backward through only the
results actually needed: indispensable lemmas, propositions, estimates,
constructions, definitions, case splits, and external inputs. Unify shared
dependencies. Exclude motivation, examples, remarks, and independent results
unless the proof spine invokes them.

Represent every essential obligation as a node and every actual use as an edge
from dependency to consumer. Every non-root node must lie on a path to a
headline claim. For every node verify the precise statement proved. For every
edge check conclusion strength, hypotheses, parameter ranges and uniformity,
normalizations, quantifier order, variable binding, and logical sufficiency.
Detect missing dependencies, cycles, silent strengthening, disconnected
constructions, and final deductions that do not follow. Continue through every
headline claim after finding a defect.

Assign each claim exactly one status:
- established: every essential node and edge was verified;
- repairably_incomplete: a precise essential gap has a credible repair inside
  the paper's method;
- method_breakdown: a precise essential failure has no credible in-method
  repair; or
- unresolved: the available source does not support a responsible judgment.

For edges, verified permits only satisfied or inapplicable checks;
repairably_incomplete and failed each require at least one not_satisfied check
and no unresolved check; unresolved requires at least one unresolved check and
no definite not_satisfied check. A repairably incomplete item links only
repairable-gap findings. A failed item links only unsalvageable findings.

Create a verifier-output finding for every failed or incomplete essential node
or edge, and put its ID in that node or edge's finding_ids. Every verifier
finding must be linked from at least one failed or incomplete graph item;
verified and unresolved graph items link none. Unresolved checks belong in both
artifacts' coverage notes rather than being manufactured into defects.
Established means only that the submitted
spine closes under its explicitly imported inputs; it is not proof of theorem
truth beyond the audit.
